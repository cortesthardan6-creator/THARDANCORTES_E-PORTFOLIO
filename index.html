LESSON FOR GRADE 7 LEARNERS

ELEMENTS OF SHORT STORY

01

CHARACTER

The People (or beings) in the story who take part the events

02

THEME

The underlying message or central idea of the story.

03

STYLE

The author's unique way of writing, including the use of language, tone, and literary devices.

04

PLOT

The sequence of events in the story, including the beginning, rising action, climax, falling action and resolution.

05

SETTING

The time and place where the story happens

06

POINT OF VIEW

The perspective from which the story is told(first person, second person, or third person).
